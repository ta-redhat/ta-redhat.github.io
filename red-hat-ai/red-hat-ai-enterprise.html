Red Hat AI Enterprise

What is it?

Red Hat AI Enterprise is an integrated, ready-to-use AI platform for building, developing, and deploying AI-powered applications, agentic workflows, and efficient AI models across the hybrid cloud, all under one subscription.

It's the "everything bundled" tier of the Red Hat AI portfolio — combining Red Hat OpenShift AI, the underlying Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform, and unlimited Red Hat AI Accelerator entitlements into a single product, so an organization doesn't have to separately license and integrate each layer itself.

How does it work?

Because it bundles OpenShift AI with OpenShift Container Platform, AI Enterprise gives your team one unified environment to fine-tune models, build and manage agentic AI workflows, and serve AI-powered applications with high throughput and low latency — with intelligent scaling handled by the platform. Layered security and safety controls are built in, so you can run workloads across hybrid cloud environments without each team re-solving governance from scratch. Because the AI accelerator (GPU) entitlements are unlimited under this SKU, teams don't need to track and license accelerator usage separately as they scale.

Why do I need it?

Assembling your own AI platform means stitching together several layers before you've even started building anything:

  • A Kubernetes layer
  • An MLOps/GenAIOps layer
  • Inference optimization
  • Governance
  • Accelerator licensing

AI Enterprise replaces that assembly work with one supported, pre-integrated platform — most valuable when scaling from a handful of AI experiments to many production workloads.

Where do I get it?

Red Hat AI Enterprise is not sold through the self-serve Red Hat Store — it's billed per node as an enterprise subscription. Contact Red Hat sales: https://www.redhat.com/en/contact.

AWS Marketplace pricing (listing): usage-based at $5.94/hour, consistent across the supported GPU instance types (p5en.48xlarge, p6-b200.48xlarge, p5.48xlarge, p4d.24xlarge). AWS infrastructure costs are billed separately.
