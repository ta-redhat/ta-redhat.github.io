# Red Hat OpenShift Platform Plus

## What is it?
**Red Hat OpenShift Platform Plus is the most complete self-managed OpenShift edition — OpenShift Container Platform plus multicluster management, security, registry, and storage, bundled into one subscription.**

It's Red Hat's recommended choice for organizations that want the full application platform experience without separately sourcing and integrating cluster management, security, and registry tooling on their own.

## How does it work?
Platform Plus includes everything in OpenShift Container Platform, plus **Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes** (fleet-wide visibility and governance from one console), **Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security for Kubernetes** (Kubernetes-native security across build/deploy/runtime), **Red Hat Quay** (a scalable central container registry), and **Red Hat OpenShift Data Foundation Essentials** (persistent storage/data services for clusters). You install OpenShift Container Platform first, then layer in Advanced Cluster Management, which can apply policy sets to install the remaining components — giving you one consistent management and security framework across every cluster, wherever it runs.

## Why do I need it?
A single OpenShift cluster can't do it all — real enterprise use needs multicluster management, security, and registry capabilities, and sourcing those separately means extra procurement and inconsistent tooling. Platform Plus bundles what most organizations need anyway into one subscription with one support relationship.

## Where do I get it?
Not sold through the self-serve Red Hat Store; pricing varies by sizing and subscription choice. **Contact Red Hat sales**: [https://www.redhat.com/en/contact](https://www.redhat.com/en/contact).

**AWS Marketplace pricing for ROSA specifically**: a dedicated ["Red Hat OpenShift Platform Plus for ROSA" listing](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-dm5otbp2o25zk) (requires an existing ROSA subscription) publishes **$87.50 per 12 months** per worker-node vCPU unit for managed clusters, or **$275.00 per 12 months** per unit for self-managed clusters, with usage-based overage rates. General self-managed (non-ROSA) Platform Plus pricing remains sizing-dependent and is not publicly listed — contact sales for a quote.
