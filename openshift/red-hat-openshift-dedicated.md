# Red Hat OpenShift Dedicated — What / How / Why / Where

## What is it?
**Red Hat OpenShift Dedicated is a fully managed OpenShift offering, hosted on AWS or Google Cloud, where Red Hat's own site reliability engineering (SRE) team runs the cluster for you.**

It's for organizations that want OpenShift's capabilities without taking on the operational burden of installing, upgrading, and maintaining the platform themselves.

## How does it work?
Red Hat Operations manages the full lifecycle of your cluster — installation, monitoring, platform upgrades, and security patches — backed by a 99.95% uptime SLA and 24x7 Red Hat Premium Support. You choose standard, high-memory, or high-CPU worker nodes, and pricing is based on node sizing, number of availability zones, and whether you use the Standard model (Red Hat manages billing with the cloud vendor on your behalf) or Customer Cloud Subscription (you're billed directly by the cloud vendor). Purchased through Google Cloud Marketplace, it counts toward your existing Google Cloud committed-use discounts and arrives on a single consolidated invoice.

## Why do I need it?
Running OpenShift yourself means your team owns every upgrade, patch, and operational incident on the platform layer — time and expertise that could otherwise go toward the applications actually running on it. OpenShift Dedicated removes that operational burden by having Red Hat's own SRE team run the platform, which is most valuable for teams that want OpenShift's consistency and feature set but would rather focus entirely on building and scaling their applications than on managing cluster infrastructure.

## Where do I get it?
Not sold through the self-serve Red Hat Store. Purchase directly through Red Hat, via the Google Cloud Marketplace, or **contact Red Hat sales**: [https://www.redhat.com/en/contact](https://www.redhat.com/en/contact).

**Published pricing** ([redhat.com/.../openshift/dedicated](https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/cloud-computing/openshift/dedicated)): worker node vCPU costs **$0.171/4vCPU/hour** on hourly/on-demand billing (~$1,500/year equivalent), **$0.114/4vCPU/hour** with a 1-year commitment (~$1,000/year), or **$0.076/4vCPU/hour** with a 3-year commitment (~$667/year). Purchasing through the Google Cloud Marketplace gives a single combined invoice for the Red Hat OpenShift license and Google Cloud infrastructure, and counts toward existing Google Cloud committed use discounts (CUDs).
