# Red Hat Developer Hub — What / How / Why / Where

## What is it?
**Red Hat Developer Hub is an enterprise-grade internal developer portal, built on the open source Backstage project, that gives development teams one centralized, self-service place to find approved software templates, documentation, tools, and best practices.**

It's meant to replace the DIY approach many organizations take to building their own internal developer portal — delivering a fully supported, turnkey version of that experience instead.

## How does it work?
Developer Hub centralizes technology resources and "golden path" software templates — pre-approved combinations of infrastructure, application services, toolchains, and policy — so developers (and increasingly, AI coding agents) can get started on a new project quickly and consistently, following organizational standards without having to look them up each time. It supports enterprise role-based access control (RBAC) and compliance policy enforcement, configurable without sacrificing the simplicity of the developer-facing experience, and is extensible through a library of plug-ins (including Red Hat's own plug-ins for Backstage) that integrate with tools like OpenShift, CI/CD systems, and the rest of the Red Hat Trusted Software Supply Chain. It deploys on Red Hat OpenShift, Amazon EKS, Microsoft Azure AKS, or other Kubernetes platforms.

## Why do I need it?
As an engineering organization grows, developers waste real time just figuring out which tools, templates, and internal standards to use for a new service — and without a shared portal, every team tends to reinvent its own (inconsistent) approach. Developer Hub solves that by giving everyone one place to start from, enforcing governance and best practices by default rather than relying on developers to remember or look them up, which reduces onboarding friction and keeps new projects aligned with organizational standards from day one.
