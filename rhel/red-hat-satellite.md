# Red Hat Satellite — What / How / Why / Where

## What is it?
**Red Hat Satellite is the premier management add-on for Red Hat Enterprise Linux, handling the provisioning, patching, configuration, and security compliance of your entire RHEL footprint from a single console.**

Where RHEL is the operating system itself, Satellite is the tool IT teams use to manage potentially thousands of RHEL systems at once — from bare-metal discovery and provisioning, to patch and content distribution, to ongoing configuration and compliance monitoring — across on-premise, cloud, and disconnected/air-gapped environments.

## How does it work?
Satellite acts as a centralized hub: it syncs and verifies Red Hat's software content (with digital-signature checks for supply chain trust), then lets you curate specific repositories and patches for Dev, QA, and Production environments before rolling them out on your own schedule. For new hardware, it automates discovery and provisioning with standardized compute profiles and integrates DNS/DHCP/identity services. On an ongoing basis, it runs automated configuration checks, flags security/compliance drift, and prioritizes vulnerabilities by risk — all viewable and actionable from one console, even offline via its local knowledge portal.

## Why do I need it?
Patching and provisioning a handful of RHEL servers by hand is manageable; doing it for a large, distributed fleet without a central tool means inconsistent patch levels, slower vulnerability response, and no single source of truth for compliance. Satellite solves that by giving you one place to control updates at scale, on your own schedule, with verifiable supply-chain trust and full visibility into your organization's security posture — including in air-gapped environments where you can't just reach out to the internet for patches.
